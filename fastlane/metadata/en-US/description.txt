Church Presenter is the mobile companion for the ChurchPresenter desktop app — and it can also run a service on its own. Project songs, Bible passages, pictures, slides and video from anywhere in the room.

THREE WAYS TO PRESENT
• Remote control — drive ChurchPresenter on your church computer over Wi-Fi. The computer shows the slides; your phone is the remote.
• Standalone — your phone or tablet is the presenter. Send slides to a TV with AirPlay or a USB-C or Lightning adapter. No computer and no internet needed.
• Calendar — plan services on your phone during the week. Plans reach the church computer over the cloud, and every enrolled phone sees the same plan.

PROJECT TO SCREEN INSTANTLY
• Songs — search by number or title, filter by songbook, project lyrics verse by verse
• Bible — jump to any book, chapter and verse and put it on screen with one tap
• Pictures — browse image folders and project any photo
• Presentations — show slide decks and step through them
• Media — play video and audio from the computer, a network URL or your phone, with play, pause and seek controls
• Clear the screen at any moment

BUILD THE SERVICE SCHEDULE
Add songs, Bible readings, pictures, presentations and media to the schedule from your phone. It stays in sync with the desktop in real time.

LIVE Q&A
Moderate audience questions: approve or deny them, edit them, put one on screen and mark it done.

YOUR OWN LIBRARY
Write songs and announcements on your phone, copy Bible translations from your computer over Wi-Fi, choose a backdrop and text size, and export a CCLI song usage report — all without internet.

SIMPLE SETUP
1. Run ChurchPresenter on the computer connected to your projector
2. Put your iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi network
3. Scan the QR code shown by the desktop app, or enter the address by hand
4. Start presenting

No account or login required. Designed for iPhone and iPad, available in 35 languages.

REQUIREMENTS
• Remote control and calendar modes need the ChurchPresenter desktop app
• iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.2 or later
